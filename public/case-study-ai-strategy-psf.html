Case Study: AI Strategy for P.S.O.F

Founder: 17-year-old student entrepreneur from Karachi.

Product: P.S.O.F

Challenge

Needed a fast, private way to structure strategy without onboarding complex tools or sharing sensitive product details.

Approach

Used the ShadowTalk AI strategy agent as a private consultant:

  • Mapped goals, users, and risks in one session
  • Generated a 90-day execution plan with weekly check-ins
  • Kept everything local to the chat; no account required
“I treated ShadowTalk like a strategy partner, not a chatbot. The no-login, privacy-first setup mattered.”

Outcome

Built a clearer launch roadmap, faster stakeholder alignment, and a repeatable weekly planning rhythm.
