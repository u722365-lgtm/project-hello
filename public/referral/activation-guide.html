ShadowTalk Referral Activation Guide

Share ShadowTalk with friends, students, and founders — and earn Pro credits. This is a 4-step plan you can finish in 5 minutes.

If you haven’t already: open ShadowTalk, go to the Referral Program panel, copy your referral link, and keep it handy for the prompts below.

Step-by-step activation

1
Set up your share kit

Copy your referral link, then use the ready-made messages in the “Share templates” page. Keep 2–3 variants ready so outreach doesn’t feel repetitive.

2
Choose 3 high-intent audiences

Focus on 2–3 groups where privacy matters: university students, freelance developers, and startup founders. One quality conversation beats 20 random posts.

3
Send 5 personalized introductions today

Message people who already talk about tools, productivity, or privacy. Mention why you switched to ShadowTalk and send the short version from the templates page.

4
Follow up within 48 hours

Share one result: a research summary, an offline demo, or a short proof point. Weak follow-up is the #1 reason referrals stop working.

Recommended channels

  • WhatsApp groups focused on campus tech, startups, or privacy
  • LinkedIn/Instagram DMs with founders or engineering leads
  • University clubs and AI/tech community chats
  • 1:1 asks to advisors, mentors, or former teammates

Fast copy-paste share CTAs

Use these lines for quick conversion.

Email / DM: “I’ve been using ShadowTalk for private AI research and offline developer work. If you want to try it, use my link and we’ll both get credit.”

Social / Broadcast: “Testing a new privacy-first AI workspace that runs offline and doesn’t train on your data. Free to try:”
