# ChatGPT Alternative: ShadowTalk AI

## Why Modern Users Are Ditching Mainstream AI Chat

For many teams and individuals, the default “just use ChatGPT” habit is wearing thin. The problem is not model capability—it is what happens after you send a prompt. Conversations get logged, training pipelines may absorb your data, corporate filters can inject review layers into your workflow, and pricing changes without warning. If you rely on AI for sensitive strategy, research notes, or outbound customer conversations, those defaults become active risks.

What most users actually want from an AI chat alternative is simple: keep the intelligence, remove the exposure.

---

## What ShadowTalk AI Solves

ShadowTalk AI is built around three non-negotiable constraints:

1. **Local-first storage** — message history stays in the app by default and is not synced to external training corpora.  
2. **End-to-end encrypted messaging** — each message is encrypted using AES-256-GCM before leaving the client, with keys derivable from user-managed secrets.  
3. **No training on user data** — ShadowTalk does not opt user conversations into model improvement without explicit opt-in, reversing the default behavior of many mainstream platforms.

That combination removes the three biggest friction sources in ChatGPT alternatives: privacy anxiety, loss of context over time, and unpredictable commercial terms.
