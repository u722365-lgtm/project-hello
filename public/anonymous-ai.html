Anonymous AI — No Account, No Login, No Training

Most AI tools tie you to an account, track your prompts, and train models from your conversations. ShadowTalk AI rejects that model.

Anonymous AI in practice: open ShadowTalk AI, start chatting, and close the tab. No accounts. No login. No data sent for training.

Why anonymous AI matters

  • No account gate — access AI instantly without identifiers
  • No prompt training — your conversations stay outside model training pipelines
  • Encrypted sessions — context is protected by default
  • Local model option — run offline models without sending data anywhere
  • Short memory window — auto-expires chat context to reduce exposure

How to use anonymous AI safely

  1. Open ShadowTalk AI with no login required
  2. Use an encrypted session for sensitive prompts
  3. Avoid pasting personal identifiers unless necessary
  4. Use local models when absolute isolation is required
  5. Close the session when finished — no handoff or sync queue
