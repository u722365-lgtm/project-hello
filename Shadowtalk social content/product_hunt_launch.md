# Product Hunt Launch Copy for ShadowTalk

## Title
ShadowTalk AI

## Tagline
Privacy-first AI that forgets you. Built for the paranoid.

## Description (Maker's Comment)
Hey Product Hunt! 👋 I’m Zain, the 17-year-old solo founder behind ShadowTalk AI.

I built ShadowTalk because I realized something terrifying: every time you talk to ChatGPT or Claude, your thoughts, code, and private plans are stored on their servers and often used as training data. 

I wanted an AI where the conversation fades when you want it to. A "burn-after-reading" AI. 

So I built it.

ShadowTalk isn't just another wrapper. It’s built from the ground up with strict Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) principles to be the most distraction-free, privacy-centric AI platform on the internet. 

**Features:**
🔒 **Zero-Retention Privacy:** When you delete it, it's actually gone. No shadow databases.
🔍 **Autonomous Deep Research:** Let the AI browse and synthesize hours of research into minutes.
🎙️ **Voice Command System:** Hands-free, fluid interaction.
💻 **Integrated IDE:** Code alongside your AI without switching contexts.
⚡ **HCI-First Design:** Blazing fast, beautiful, and distraction-free.

I built this entirely on my own from Karachi, Pakistan, optimizing the architecture to keep server costs at absolute zero while handling global traffic, and engineering custom local payment gateways since Stripe isn't available here. 

I’d love for you to try it out for your most sensitive writing, coding, or brainstorming. Let me know what you think of the UX and the privacy features! 

I'll be hanging out in the comments all day to answer your questions! 🚀
